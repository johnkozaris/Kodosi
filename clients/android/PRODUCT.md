# Kodosi for Android

[PRODUCT.md](../../PRODUCT.md) has the shared intent and [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) the
visual system.

The app follows and controls the terminals of the person's computers and of the friends
who share with them. It hosts nothing, and its screens follow the
[iPhone app](../ios/PRODUCT.md).

- The phone sets the size of a terminal only while it has focus.
- The app's data stays out of backups and device transfers.
