# Kodosi for iPhone and iPad

Shared product intent and current behavior: [Kodosi's PRODUCT.md](../../PRODUCT.md).
Visual system: [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## What the app does

The app follows, answers, and controls the terminals that run on your computers and on
the computers of the people who share with you. Starting a terminal or a program,
changing who a terminal is shared with, and reading provider conversations stay on the
computer.

## Words

The words are the Mac's: terminal, room, friend, agent, device. Session, host,
runtime, and mission are internal words.

## Sign in and trust

Sign-in shows the device code and opens the sign-in page in the app. A new iPhone or
iPad is trusted like a new computer: a device that you already trust enters its code,
or you use your recovery key, or you start fresh.

## Terminals

The Terminals tab groups your terminals by computer, then lists the terminals that
friends share with you. Each row shows the sign of its state, which the host computer
sends through the server. The agent's question shows when you open the terminal. The
tab shows the number of your own terminals that wait for you.

A terminal opens in the real terminal view. The phone controls the terminal size only
while it holds focus; another view can take it back. A key row gives the keys a phone
lacks, and 1, 2 and 3 answer agent menus. The composer sends a message as one paste,
and Return only when you tap send. A hardware keyboard types into the terminal.

## Rooms and people

Rooms show the conversation, tasks, and terminals of each room. On a wide iPad they
sit side by side. People shows friend requests first, then friends and the terminals
they share. Settings holds the account, devices, the recovery key, appearance,
licenses, and account deletion.

## iPhone and iPad experience

The terminal stays central and the controls stay calm. Use the system tab bar,
sheets, swipe actions, and context menus, with Kodosi's colors and motion. Support
Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, reduced motion, landscape, and iPad windows.
