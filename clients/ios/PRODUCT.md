# Kodosi for iPhone and iPad

Shared intent: [PRODUCT.md](../../PRODUCT.md). Visual system: [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

The app follows, answers and controls the terminals of your computers and of the
friends who share with you, and takes part in rooms. Starting terminals or programs,
sharing a terminal with friends and reading provider conversations stay on the
computer.

- Sign-in opens in a system sign-in window. A new device is trusted like a computer: by
  a code entered on a trusted device, the recovery key, or starting fresh.
- The Terminals tab groups your terminals by computer, then friends' terminals, each
  with the sign of its state, and counts the waiting terminals that you can answer: your
  own, and others' while you have them open.
- In a terminal, the phone sets the size only while it has focus, and never to fewer
  than 10 rows: with less room, the terminal shows its bottom rows. A key row adds the
  keys a phone lacks; 1, 2 and 3 answer agent menus. The composer sends text as one
  paste and Return only when you tap send. A hardware keyboard types directly.
- Rooms show the conversation, tasks and terminals, side by side on a wide iPad, and the
  Rooms tab counts invitations and rooms with new messages. Friends lists requests first,
  then friends and their terminals. Settings holds the account, devices, recovery key,
  appearance, licenses and account deletion.
