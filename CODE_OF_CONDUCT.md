# Code of Conduct

Kodosi is a place where people work together. This project is the same. Everyone who
takes part in its issues, pull requests, and reviews agrees to these rules.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume that the other person means well.
- Talk about the work: the code, the design, the behavior. Do not attack the person.
- Give reasons with your opinion, and accept that others can disagree.
- Accept correction, and say so when you made a mistake.
- Keep private data private: no terminal content, credentials, or personal details of
  other people in issues, logs, or pictures.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, harassment, or threats, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or images, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Remarks that demean a person for who they are: their age, body, disability,
  ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race,
  religion, or sexual identity or orientation.
- Publication of private information of another person without permission.
- Conduct that a reasonable person would find out of place in professional work.

## Where this applies

In this repository and in each place where a person speaks for the project.

## Report a problem

Contact the maintainer in private through the details on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/johnkozaris). Say what occurred,
where, and when, and add links if you have them. The maintainer keeps the identity of
the reporter private.

## What the maintainer does

The maintainer reads each report and decides in a fair way. The result can be a
private note, a warning, the removal of a comment or a contribution, or a temporary
or permanent ban from the project.

## Source

These rules follow the ideas of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, in shorter words.
