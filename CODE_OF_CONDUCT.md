# Code of Conduct

Kodosi follows the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
in its issues, pull requests, reviews, and wherever someone speaks for the project. Be
kind, discuss the work rather than the person, and keep other people's private data
private.

Report a problem in private to [hello@kodosi.com](mailto:hello@kodosi.com) with what
happened, where and when. The maintainers keep reporters' identities private and may
respond with a note, a warning, removal of content, or a temporary or permanent ban.
