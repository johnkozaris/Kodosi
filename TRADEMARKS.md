This page tells you how you can use them.

## Uses that need no permission

- Build, change and run Kodosi for yourself, your team or your organization, also on a
  service that you run for them. The name and the logo can stay in the apps, on the
  sign-in pages and in the e-mails. Your own server addresses and sign-in settings are
  configuration, not a change.
- Say that your work uses Kodosi, works with Kodosi or comes from Kodosi, when this is
  true. For example: "a fork of Kodosi" or "works with Kodosi".
- Write about Kodosi, review it, teach it and show pictures of it.
- Send changes to this repository.

## Uses that need written permission

- Give a changed version of Kodosi to other people with the name Kodosi or with the
  Kodosi logo.
- Run a service for people outside your team or organization with the name Kodosi or
  with the Kodosi logo, for example with the Kodosi sign-in pages.
- Say or suggest that the Kodosi project runs, checks or supports your service or your
  version.
- Use the name Kodosi, or a name or a logo that people can confuse with it, in the name
  of a product, a company, a domain or an entry in an app store.
- Sell goods with the name or the logo.

## A fork or a service for other people

- Give it a different name, a different logo and different app icons.
- Remove the name and the logo from the apps, the sign-in pages and the e-mails.
- You can say that it is based on Kodosi.
